## Firecrawl Platform & Service Plans
The CLI interacts with the official Firecrawl API ([firecrawl.dev](https://www.firecrawl.dev)) or self-hosted instances:
- **Free / Keyless Tier**: Up to 500 free credits upon signup for testing and small scripts.
- **Starter & Scale Plans**: Monthly subscription tiers providing higher concurrency, priority queues, and higher rate limits.
- **Self-Hosted Open Source**: Firecrawl is open source; you can run the Firecrawl engine locally via Docker and point `FIRECRAWL_API_URL` to your instance with zero subscription costs.

For official Firecrawl cloud pricing and credit allowances, visit [https://www.firecrawl.dev/pricing](https://www.firecrawl.dev/pricing).
